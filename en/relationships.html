Relationships tarot spread

Five cards about two people: what each of you brings, where the strength is, where the weakness is, and what the connection is moving toward.

Beginners usually start without reversed cards.

What the positions mean

#Position
1You
2Partner
3Strength
4Weakness
5Perspective

How to read it

Compare cards 3 and 4 — strength and weakness are usually two sides of the same thing.
