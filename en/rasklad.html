How to read three cards

  • Read each card by its position first — that is the base.
  • Then look at them together: how many Major Arcana, which suits dominate.
  • Ask yourself what actually resonates. The second meaning often arrives later.
  • Do not repeat the same question the same day. The answer will not become "better".

Common mistakes

  • Asking a yes/no question. Three cards answer "how" and "why", not "will it happen".
  • Reading only the "bad" card. A difficult card marks where attention is needed.
  • Drawing again until you like it. That is not a reading, it is bargaining.
