The three-card spread: 5 layouts and how to read them

28 September 2026

Three cards are the most universal spread. It is fast, concrete and does not overload a beginner. The main thing is to decide in advance what each position means.

Five workable layouts

  • The overall situation: Past — Present — Future
  • What to do: Situation — Obstacle — Advice
  • Relationships: You — Partner — What is between you
  • Work and money: Now — What gets in the way — Where it leads
  • Me and the goal: What gets in the way — My resource — The first step

The order of reading

  • First read each card in its position — that is the base.
  • Then look at the three as a whole: how many Major Arcana, which suits prevail, whether anything repeats.
  • At the end ask yourself what resonates most. The first honest thought is usually more accurate than a textbook.

The whole picture

  • Two or more Major Arcana — the situation matters, you are at a turning point.
  • Only Minor Arcana — much is decided by concrete actions, not by fate.
  • Three cards of one suit — the theme of that element has come to the fore: feelings, actions, thoughts or money.
  • All three cards reversed — most likely you are looking at the situation from the wrong side, or putting off a decision.

Common mistakes

  • Changing the question after an inconvenient answer. This is not fortune-telling for what you want, it is a conversation.
  • Reading a card outside its position: the Five of Cups in the “resource” position and in the “obstacle” position mean different things.
  • Expecting literal events. A card describes energy and a tendency, not a date and an address.

If you want to get to know a particular card, open the reference of meanings: upright and reversed, advice and a breakdown by topic.

When three cards stop being enough, look through all the spreads: the Celtic Cross of 10 cards, the year ahead spread, “Mirror” and “Path”.
