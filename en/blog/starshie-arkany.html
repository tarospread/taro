The Major and Minor Arcana: how a 78-card deck is built

29 September 2026

The deck has 78 cards, and they fall into two large groups.

The Major Arcana — 22 cards

From “the Fool” (0) to “the World” (XXI). This is the hero's journey: important stages, fateful events, durable archetypes. When a spread holds many Major Arcana, the matter is not a household trifle but a significant period.

The Minor Arcana — 56 cards

Four suits of 14 cards each: from Ace to Ten plus four court cards.

SuitElementSphere
WandsFireEnergy, action, career, creativity
CupsWaterFeelings, relationships, intuition
SwordsAirThoughts, decisions, conflicts, truth
PentaclesEarthMoney, work, body, the material world

Numbers matter

  • Ace — pure potential, a beginning
  • 2 — balance, choice, union
  • 3 — growth, the first result
  • 4 — stability, taking root
  • 5 — conflict, crisis
  • 6 — harmony, communication
  • 7 — challenge, assessment, defence
  • 8 — movement, progress, mastery
  • 9 — almost the finale, the culmination
  • 10 — completion, the limit, the sum

This logic works across all four suits: the Five is always about tension, the Six about restored exchange, the Ten about the end of a cycle.

Court cards

  • Page — a beginning, a student, a message
  • Knight — action, movement, a mission
  • Queen — maturity, inner strength, intuition
  • King — mastery, authority, outward control

A court card can be read both as a person in your surroundings and as your own role in the situation.

Combinations of elements

  • Fire and Water — steam: hot action plus feeling
  • Fire and Earth — lava: energy frozen into matter
  • Air and Water — fog: thoughts tangled in emotions
  • Earth and Air — dust: calculation arguing with reality

Understanding the structure gives more than memorising 78 meanings: knowing the suit and the number, you already read half the card.
