Celtic Cross tarot spread

The most famous large spread. Ten positions describe a whole situation: what stands in the centre, what blocks it, what surrounds you and where it leads.

Beginners usually start without reversed cards.

What the positions mean

#Position
1Situation
2Obstacle
3Foundation
4Recent past
5Possibility
6Near future
7Your position
8Environment
9Hopes and fears
10Outcome

How to read it

Start with cards 1–2 (the centre), then read around the cross, finish with the outcome.
