Mirror tarot spread

Four cards as a mirror: the outside view, the inside state, the strength you can rely on and the zone that wants to grow.

Beginners usually start without reversed cards.

What the positions mean

#Position
1How others see you
2What is inside
3Strengths
4Growth zone

How to read it

Compare card 1 and card 2 — the gap between them is the most useful part of this spread.
