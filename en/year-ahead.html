Year ahead tarot spread

Twelve cards, one per month. Not a forecast of events, but a map of themes: where the year will be heavy and where it will be light.

Beginners usually start without reversed cards.

How to read it

Look at the sequence of suits and Majors — that is the year’s storyline.
