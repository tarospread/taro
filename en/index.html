What this site is

A quiet tarot deck with no account, no subscription and no upsell. The daily card is the same for every visitor and changes once a day. The three-card spread is individual: a question, three positions, a reading you can come back to.

We use the Rider–Waite–Smith deck (1909). Meanings are written for reflection, not for fortune-telling: a card names a theme, and you decide what to do with it.

How to ask a question

Open questions work, yes/no questions do not. "What do I need to understand about this conversation?" gives a card something to answer. "Will he call?" only gives you your own wish back.

Frequently asked

Is it free?
Yes. No registration, no paid spreads.

Do you store my questions?
No. The question stays in your browser; only anonymous visit statistics are collected.

Can I use reversed cards?
Yes, there is a switch. Beginners usually start without them.
