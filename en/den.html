Why a daily reading works

The day is the shortest cycle you can actually check. In the evening you can see whether the card's theme appeared in your day — not "someday". That is why a daily card is the best practice for a beginner: one card, one day, one conclusion.

How to do it

  • In the morning, before the first tasks. Two quiet minutes are enough.
  • One question per day. "What do I need to notice today?" works better than "What will happen?".
  • One card. If you want detail, add "what helps" and "what blocks".
  • Write it down. One line: the card, your first thought.
  • Check in the evening. Not "did it come true", but "where did I see this today".

What not to do

  • Do not re-draw. A second draw on the same day is not an answer, it is adjusting the card to your wish.
  • Do not treat it as a verdict. A "bad" card is a theme, not a fate.
  • Do not outsource the decision. The decision stays with you.
